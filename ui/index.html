THE DATA BEHIND THE ANSWERS

A marketplace you can investigate.

Olist's historical Brazilian ecommerce dataset connects approximately 99,000 orders with their items, payments, customers, sellers, products, delivery dates, and reviews across nine source files.

Reporting includes delivered orders purchased from January 2017 through August 2018. Currency is Brazilian real (BRL). Merchandise value excludes freight and is not profit or corporate revenue.

From question to evidence

  1. Choose an investigation and its dates, or ask a supported question.
  2. Specialists retrieve reporting data or definitions and prepare the analysis.
  3. Inspect the findings, charts, source evidence, and verification results.

This is not a live store feed. CAUSYN does not forecast, prove causes, or know refund policies. Seller identifiers are anonymized. Document retrieval uses keyword search.

Recorded examples make no API calls. Live runs a new, limited investigation. Checks and AI review reduce errors but do not guarantee correctness. Each question is independent.
